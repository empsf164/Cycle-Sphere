Brand Heritage

Find Your Ride.
Define Your Journey.

Cycle Sphere was founded on a singular conviction: that a bicycle should never simply be an assembly of off-the-shelf components, but a unified extension of human vitality and mechanical harmony.

Our Atelier Story

From Alpine Passes to Global Pelotons

Born in 2018 in a modest workshop at the foothills of the Colorado Rockies, our founders—former pro road riders, composite aerospace engineers, and lifelong trail builders—set out to eliminate the compromises found in mass-market cycling retail.

We rejected anonymous contract manufacturing in favor of proprietary monocoque mold designs, laser-aligned frame jigs, and rigorous dyno load testing. Every millimeter of our Toray carbon layup and hydroformed tubing is tuned to dissipate fatiguing high-frequency road buzz while delivering direct, instantaneous forward propulsion.

Core Tenets

The Four Pillars of Cycle Sphere

1. Unyielding Material Integrity

We utilize only genuine Toray T800/T1000 carbon fiber and custom double-butted 6061 alloy with full tensile batch traceability.

2. 100% Pre-Assembly Calibration

No bike arrives in a jumble of raw parts. Every gear indexed, brake bled, and bolt torqued to specification by our certified mechanics.

3. 30-Day Trail Confidence

Real riding conditions are the only true fit test. If you are not thoroughly captivated by your ride within 30 days, return or exchange it.

4. Lifelong Rider Concierge

From remote geometry consultations to replacement hanger dispatch, our rider support network is available throughout your journey.
