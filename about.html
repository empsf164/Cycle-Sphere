Brand Philosophy

Find Your Ride.
Define Your Journey.

Cycle Sphere was founded on a singular conviction: that a bicycle should never simply be an assembly of generic parts, but a harmonized instrument of human freedom, mechanical precision, and outdoor exploration.

Our Atelier Story

From Alpine Trails to Global Pelotons

Born in 2018 in a specialized engineering workshop at the base of the Colorado Rockies, our team—composed of former UCI road racers, composite aerospace engineers, and dedicated trail builders—set out to eradicate the compromises pervasive in mass-market cycling retail.

We rejected third-party catalogue frames in favor of proprietary monocoque mold designs, laser-aligned frame jigs, and rigorous dyno load testing. Every millimeter of our Toray carbon layup and hydroformed tubing is tuned to absorb fatiguing high-frequency road vibrations while delivering direct, instantaneous forward acceleration.

Core Tenets

The Four Pillars of Cycle Sphere

The non-negotiable principles behind every road machine, trail conqueror, and urban commuter we calibrate.

1. Unyielding Material Integrity

We utilize only certified high-modulus Toray T800 and T1000 carbon fiber along with double-butted 6061-T6 alloy with full tensile batch traceability.

2. 100% Pre-Assembly

No bicycle arrives in a jumble of raw parts. Every gear indexed, brake caliper bled, and bolt torqued to specification by our certified mechanics.

3. 30-Day Trail Confidence

Real riding terrain is the only true fit test. If you are not completely satisfied by fit or feel within 30 days, return or exchange it hassle-free.

4. Lifelong Rider Concierge

From remote geometry consultations to replacement component dispatch, our rider support network is available throughout your journey.

Engineering Precision

Hand-Calibrated for Longevity & Speed

We inspect spoke tension with digital gauges, bleed hydraulic lines with zero micro-bubbles, and test wireless Di2 pairing prior to packing in our bespoke flight crates.
