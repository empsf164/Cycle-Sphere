How to Accurately Measure Your Inseam

Your inseam (inside leg measurement) is the single most critical factor for determining correct standover clearance.

1

Barefoot Stance

Stand upright against a flat wall with your cycling socks on. Keep your feet approximately 6 inches (15cm) apart.

2

Book Placement

Place a hardcover book between your legs with the spine pressed firmly against your pubic bone, mimicking saddle pressure.

3

Measure Distance

Measure vertically from the top of the book spine down to the floor in centimeters. This is your precise cycling inseam.

Anatomy of Speed

Core Geometry Dimensions Explained

Stack: Vertical height from the bottom bracket center to the top center of the headtube. Governs how upright or aggressive your riding posture is.
Reach: Horizontal distance from the bottom bracket center forward to the headtube center. Dictates cockpit extension.
Standover Height: Clearance from top tube midpoint to the ground. You should have 1–2 inches of clearance on road bikes and 2–4 inches on mountain bikes.
Head Tube Angle: A steeper angle (e.g. 73° on Road) yields nimble, surgical cornering; a slacker angle (e.g. 64° on Mountain) provides high-speed descent stability.
